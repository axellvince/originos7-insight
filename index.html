ORIGINOS 7 INSIGHTS

OriginOS 7
Deep Dive Report

Fluidity, practical intelligence, and a wider connected experience.

01

Key insights

◎ Report synthesis

  1. 1

    The Perception of Smoothness Continues to Evolve: From the early Atomic Animations and the system-level smoothness enhancements in OriginOS 6 to the physics-based motion, interruptible interactions, and Origin Workbench in OriginOS 7, smoothness is evolving from isolated animations into a complete experience spanning performance, multitasking, and long-term use.

  2. 2

    Personal Intelligence Evolves from Feature Expansion to Controlled Execution: Previous versions focused on expanding AI tools for Q&A, search, and note-taking. With OriginOS 7, Jovi connects system apps, personal files, and computer-based tasks, while confirmations at critical steps and activity logs help AI move from “providing assistance” to “completing tasks securely.”

  3. 3

    Design and Connectivity Evolve from a Single-Device Experience to Cross-Ecosystem Collaboration: Building on its personalization and cross-device capabilities, OriginOS 7 uses light, translucent materials, and dynamic themes to unify visual feedback, while connecting phones, computers, tablets, and Apple devices so that content, hardware capabilities, and task states can flow seamlessly across platforms.

2.1 OS Smoothness

OriginOS treats touch response, window movement, and rendering efficiency as one continuous experience.

◎ Key Insights

  1. 1

    The fluid motion language runs from the lock screen into apps, so the promise is a system-wide feel rather than a single showcase animation.

  2. 2

    Spring response changes with gesture speed and direction. The quality of interruption and reversal matters as much as animation speed.

  3. 3

    Origin Workbench and Snap-Up Engine connect smoothness to multitasking and time-sensitive actions, while vivo also makes longer-term performance claims.

✳ Further Thoughts

  1. 1

    Smoothness is not simply about speed. Truly natural interactions require system animations, content transitions, and finger movements to remain perfectly synchronized.

  2. 2

    Origin Workbench is the most important showcase of the underlying technology. Performance metrics can be difficult for users to perceive directly, whereas persistent app states across multiple applications make the benefits immediately tangible.

  3. 3

    Long-lasting smoothness is a key user concern.

2.1.1 Fluid Motion

A continuous visual language

The first three clips show how system elements reshape rather than abruptly disappear: a home-screen press, a reading or editing surface, and controls in several apps. Their shapes separate and merge, keeping the next action visually connected to the last.

The point is continuity across different surfaces. Watch where a control begins, how it follows the finger, and where it settles. The wide third clip gives a broader view of the same animation language; it is part of this feature, not a separate performance claim.

Product review

Review on device: interrupt a gesture or switch apps mid-flow. The transition should remain understandable when it is not played as a perfect demo.

2.1.2 Spring Animation

Response changes with speed

Spring Animation adjusts the bounce of components and cards to the way the user moves. A quick swipe is meant to resolve crisply; a slower one comes to rest more gently.

The theme-card clip makes this visible in a familiar choice flow. Look at the movement after the swipe stops: a natural response should suggest the card's weight without delaying the next action.

Product review

Review on device: compare fast, slow, reversed, and interrupted swipes, including at reduced-motion settings.

2.1.3 Seamless Interaction

Navigation follows the gesture

This clip uses a shopping interface to demonstrate movement through content. vivo's stated goal is that vertical and horizontal navigation can change direction without a jarring handoff.

That is a different question from the spring effect above. Here the test is whether the system keeps the user's place and responds predictably when the direction changes or a swipe ends early.

Product review

Review on device: repeat the flow in third-party apps, where touch handling may differ from built-in surfaces.

2.1.4 Origin Workbench

Several tasks, one working state

Origin Workbench keeps multiple windows within reach. The clip moves among tasks while preserving their place, so switching back does not require starting over.

The value lies in a complete task: locate information in one window, use it in another, then return to the first. Window movement is the visible layer; state retention and responsiveness determine whether the workflow is useful.

Product review

Review on device: try the same sequence with the app combinations and memory tiers sold locally.

2.1.5 Sustained Smoothness & Performance

The system behind the motion

The accompanying visuals cover vivo's longer-term smoothness, scheduling, memory, and rendering claims. These explain the engineering story behind the short interaction clips, but they are evidence of stated targets rather than a substitute for local tests.

Snap-Up Engine belongs to this performance track: vivo says it recognizes ticket-sale moments and prioritizes resources through tapping and payment. Its effect cannot be inferred from the animation clips above.

Product review

Review on device: test heat, load time, interruptions, and third-party ticket flows on local networks; check the claim against the exact supported model.

2.2 Personal Intelligence

Jovi is positioned as a connected assistant for documents, calls, recordings, notes, and planning.

◎ Key Insights

  1. 1

    Positioning Upgrade: Jovi evolves from a standalone Q&A interface into a personal AI agent connecting phones and computers, shifting its core value from “providing answers” to “completing tasks.”

  2. 2

    End-to-End Capabilities: Through content understanding, task planning, Skills, and API integration, Jovi can break down user goals and execute tasks continuously across apps and devices.

  3. 3

    More Proactive Services: Snap & Go automatically recognizes itinerary information from screenshots and suggests the next step, moving AI from “passive response” to “context-driven assistance.”

✳ Further Thoughts

  1. 1

    System-Level Capabilities Are the Core Advantage of a Personal Agent: Beyond simply generating content, smartphone makers can connect system apps, personal files, device states, and computer-based tasks to create a more complete end-to-end execution loop.

  2. 2

    The More Complex the Task, the More Critical Trusted Execution Becomes: As AI begins to access files, modify content, and schedule tasks, process transparency, confirmation at critical steps, result verification, and task rollback become essential capabilities.

  3. 3

    The Most Natural AI Should Not Add Operational Burden: Rather than introducing more entry points, AI should proactively identify user needs within existing workflows—such as recording, capturing screenshots, and searching—and seamlessly complete the next step.

2.2.1 Jovi: command on phone, done on PC

A cross-device handoff

The clip begins with a request on the phone and shows the work continuing on a computer. The user asks Jovi to change a document without returning to the PC first.

The surrounding images show the phone and PC surfaces, file reading, and configurable skills. The experience should be judged by the finished artifact and its return path, including whether the user can inspect and correct what Jovi changed.

Product review

Review for Indonesia: language quality, supported PC setup, file compatibility, and recovery when either device goes offline.

2.2.2 Scheduled news updates

A brief prepared in advance

This PC clip shows Jovi setting up a scheduled task. The user specifies a topic and time, and Jovi can compile a news brief to be saved in Notes.

It is a proactive workflow rather than the phone-to-PC editing task above. The important controls are the schedule, the sources and scope of the brief, and a way to change or stop it if the topic is no longer relevant.

Product review

Review for Indonesia: local source coverage, Bahasa Indonesia summaries, and the ability to verify the original articles.

2.2.3 Recorder: transcript to insight

Make a conversation retrievable

Recorder organizes speech by speaker, links highlights to exact moments, and produces insight and summary views. The stills here show stages of that review flow; they are not another video demonstration.

Separating speakers is only the start. A useful record lets a person jump back to the source audio, correct a name or conclusion, and choose the summary format that suits the meeting.

Product review

Review for Indonesia: mixed Bahasa Indonesia and English speech, overlapping speakers, and the accuracy of action items.

2.2.4 Snap Note

A photo becomes an editable note

The clip shows a phone scanning a photographed whiteboard or page and turning it into a structured note. Multiple pages can be kept in order for later editing.

The feature addresses the gap between capturing information and using it. Check whether headings, handwriting, diagrams, and page order survive conversion, and whether the user can fix mistakes without starting over.

Product review

Review for Indonesia: handwriting, low light, mixed-language notes, and dense classroom or meeting boards.

2.2.5 Smart Call Assistant

A missed call becomes a record

The official image shows the call assistance flow. When a call cannot be answered, the assistant can respond, transcribe the exchange, and leave a summary in the call log.

This is most useful when the caller leaves a concrete request. The summary needs to distinguish what was actually said from an inferred next step, and the user should be able to return to the original record.

Product review

Review for Indonesia: caller consent, Bahasa Indonesia transcription, and how clearly an automated answer identifies itself.

2.2.6 AI Reader Mode

Long documents on a small screen

Reader Mode reformats lengthy PDF content into a mobile reading view. The official visual places the document and adapted reading surface together, making the change in layout clear.

The practical test is whether headings, tables, footnotes, and page references remain findable after reflow. A clean view is useful only if it preserves the meaning of the original document.

Product review

Review for Indonesia: mixed-language PDFs, scanned pages, and the route back to the source page.

2.2.7 AI Search

Find information by meaning

This short official clip demonstrates a search without relying on the exact file name. The accompanying image shows the search surface and result context.

A useful result should identify where the answer came from, let the user open that file or note, and make a wrong match easy to recognize.

Product review

Review for Indonesia: local-language paraphrases, indexing delay, and privacy controls for searchable files.

2.2.8 Snap & Go

A screenshot becomes a next step

The official clip shows information from a travel or event screenshot being recognized and presented as a suggested Calendar action in Origin Island.

Its value comes from removing retyping while keeping the user in control. Dates, time zones, locations, and duplicates should be checked before an event is saved.

Product review

Review for Indonesia: local ticket and itinerary formats, ambiguous dates, and the final confirmation step.

2.3 Origin Design

The interface uses reactive light and material alongside playful lock-screen personalization.

◎ Key Insights

  1. 1

    OriginOS 7 transforms translucent materials from visual decoration into a solution for readability: Dynamic Translucent Material combines adaptive refraction, layered lighting, and background-aware adjustments to create a translucent look while keeping text and controls clear.

  2. 2

    OriginOS 7 elevates light effects from visual elements to interaction feedback: Dynamic Glow integrates light and shadow into taps, loading processes, and state transitions, creating a consistent language across visual design and interaction feedback.

  3. 3

    OriginOS 7 advances personalization from static presentation to dynamic interaction: Wiggle Theme, Glint Theme, StickIt, and Pet Pals respond to touch, device orientation, music, charging, and time of day, turning system themes into interactive content with emotional appeal.

✳ Further Thoughts

  1. 1

    As translucent materials become an industry-wide trend, OriginOS 7 differentiates itself not by being “more transparent,” but by turning materials, lighting, and physics-based motion into a cohesive design language that balances readability with clear system feedback.

  2. 2

    The long-term value of personalization depends not only on the novelty of the first experience, but also on achieving the right balance among emotional expression, information readability, interaction efficiency, and power consumption.

2.3.1 Dynamic Glow

Light follows an action

The clip shows light responding to interaction and carrying the eye into the next state. The still provides a closer look at the interface treatment.

A highlight can help confirm where a touch landed, but it should not obscure nearby controls or slow a repeated action. The important detail is how quickly the effect settles.

2.3.2 Immersive Home

A home screen that changes with time

Icons and widgets shift from a brighter daytime treatment toward a softer evening appearance. The official image shows the design language across home-screen states.

The adjustment should make the screen more comfortable without changing the location or recognition of familiar apps. Readability and consistency matter when the wallpaper is busy.

2.3.3 Translucent Material · Realistic

Depth and separation

Realistic is the first of three material treatments. The official clip shows how the layered surface moves over content, while the still shows the broader design family.

Watch the contrast between the foreground controls and the content behind them. The surface needs to signal depth without making text harder to read.

2.3.4 Translucent Material · Natural

A gentler layer

Natural offers a distinct balance of translucency and background detail. Its clip isolates the treatment so the movement of panels can be compared with Realistic.

The useful setting is the one that keeps controls legible across photographs, dark areas, and dense app content, not only on a curated wallpaper.

2.3.5 Translucent Material · Soft

Less visual texture

Soft is the third treatment shown in the official material series. It reduces the emphasis of the underlying scene and gives the foreground a calmer surface.

Compare the same text and controls in all three treatments. A person should be able to choose the visual density that fits the wallpaper and their comfort.

2.3.6 Wiggle Theme

A lock screen with motion

The lock-screen wallpaper reacts to device movement. The clip shows the motion; the still shows a second wallpaper in the same family.

The effect should remain calm at a glance and should be easy to disable when motion is distracting or battery conservation matters.

2.3.7 StickIt and live updates

Personal objects, useful status

StickIt places personal stickers onto the wallpaper and Always On Display. The clip demonstrates sticker movement; the stills show the phone layout and live status such as deliveries or flights.

Personal expression and useful information share a small area. The layout needs to keep time, notifications, and live status readable rather than letting decorations crowd them out.

2.3.8 Pet Pals Theme

An animated companion

The official clip gives the lock-screen character its own moment. Its appeal depends on expressive motion that feels responsive without demanding attention every time the phone wakes.

Check whether animations can be controlled separately from essential status and how the theme behaves in reduced-motion and power-saving settings.

2.3.9 Glint Theme

Light with device tilt

Glint shifts its iridescent pattern as the phone moves. The short official clip makes the tilt response clearer than a static thumbnail can.

The visual should be predictable in everyday hand movements and should not compete with lock-screen information.

2.4 Connectivity

Office and personal devices share tasks, status, and media across ecosystem boundaries.

◎ Key Insights

  1. 1

    OriginOS 7 positions Apple ecosystem integration at the heart of its cross-platform experience: By supporting Apple Watch notifications and health data syncing, AirPods status display, iCloud file access, and collaboration with Mac and iPad, it lowers the ecosystem barrier for Apple device users switching to a vivo smartphone.

  2. 2

    Connectivity evolves from device compatibility to task continuity: vivo Office Kit, Camera Sharing, and Task Handoff connect files, notes, camera capabilities, and creative workflows, allowing content and work states to flow naturally across phones, computers, and tablets.

  3. 3

    Jovi turns cross-device connectivity into execution capability: Jovi can search for information, process files, and complete tasks across phones and computers, transforming the smartphone from a device connection point into the intelligent hub of a personal multi-device ecosystem.

✳ Further Thoughts

  1. 1

    The core value of Apple ecosystem integration is not to replace the Apple ecosystem, but to preserve users’ existing watches, earbuds, computers, and cloud data—reducing the cost of switching from an iPhone to a vivo smartphone.

  2. 2

    Cross-ecosystem competition is shifting from the number of supported devices to the depth of collaboration: Compatibility creates real user value only when notifications, data, hardware capabilities, and task states can flow continuously across devices.

2.4.1 Cross-ecosystem continuity

Devices working together

The official wide visual frames the connectivity story across devices. The promise is a task that continues naturally when the person moves from phone to a larger screen.

The handoff should be tested as a complete job: start on one device, find the same content on the other, make a change, and return without duplicate work.

2.4.2 Office Kit

Camera and task handoff

A compatible phone can act as a MacBook call camera. A photo or screenshot can also move into an iPad workflow. The two official images show these distinct Office Kit uses.

Camera quality is only one part of the first task; connection stability and setup time matter during an actual call. For the second, the measure is how quickly the transferred item becomes useful in the destination app.

Product review

Review for Indonesia: exact compatible models, software versions, and same-network or account requirements.

2.4.3 Watch and AirPods

Familiar accessories

Apple Watch information can appear in Origin Health, while AirPods connection and battery status can appear in Origin Island. Each official image shows the related surface.

These links are most useful when pairing and status stay dependable. Availability may depend on device generation and the permissions granted to the connected accessory.

2.4.4 iCloud access

Files across accounts

File Manager can reach iCloud for web after Apple Account sign-in. The official image shows the file access entry point.

The useful test is whether file names, previews, download state, and edits stay understandable when a person moves between local and cloud copies.

2.4.5 Shake & Share

A group photo handoff

Nearby phones can join a photo-sharing moment through a shared shake gesture. The official clip shows the transfer in motion, and the still shows the phone interface.

A social gesture succeeds when discovery, recipient selection, and transfer completion are clear. The exact supported devices and proximity requirements determine where it is practical.

2.5 vivo Security

The trust story pairs published assessments with visible controls for assistants, apps, and permissions.

◎ Key Insights

  1. 1

    OriginOS 7 focuses not only on what AI can access, but also on what it actually does: When Jovi carries out a task, it shows key actions, asks for confirmation before sensitive steps, and keeps an activity record for users to review.

  2. 2

    Permission management becomes more proactive: The system checks how apps use permissions such as the camera, microphone, and location, and alerts users when certain access may be unnecessary.

✳ Further Thoughts

  1. 1

    As AI begins to operate computers, edit files, and schedule tasks on the user’s behalf, asking for permission only once is no longer enough: Users also need to see what is happening and be able to confirm, stop, or undo important actions.

  2. 2

    Good security should not constantly interrupt the user: The key is to identify risks accurately, provide warnings only when needed, and keep a clear record of each action so users can stay informed without being overwhelmed by prompts.

2.5.1 Trust and certification

The wider evidence

vivo pairs its security story with transparency reporting and external assessments. The official visual collects those trust signals in one place.

Certifications are context for the product's security practices. The day-to-day test remains whether the controls on the phone are understandable and timely.

2.5.2 Jovi Security

Agent actions users can follow

Jovi Security presents the assistant's key steps and asks for confirmation when an operation is sensitive. The official image shows this control layer around agent activity.

A traceable record makes an outcome reviewable later. The confirmation should say what will change and which account or file is affected before a sensitive step proceeds.

2.5.3 Security Shield

Signals from apps and networks

Security Shield watches app installation and network connection activity for potential threats. The official image shows the feature's warning surface.

The reason for a warning needs to be specific enough to support a choice, with a clear route to inspect the app or connection involved.

2.5.4 Permission suggestions

Notice unusual access

The system can flag unusually frequent permission use and suggest a change. The official image shows how the recommendation is presented.

A good suggestion names the app and permission, explains what triggered it, and lets the person reverse a change if it interrupts a legitimate task.
